# Getting help

- **Questions and help:** our [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/5Tjw429zwN) is the fastest way.
- **Bugs:** open an issue in the repository of the project, with the log file and the steps that lead to the problem.
  For Stormworks mods the log is `%TEMP%\stormworks_mods.log`.
- **Documentation:** [DeltaLoader docs](https://deltav-modding.github.io/DeltaLoader/), [stormworks-tools.com](https://stormworks-tools.com/).
